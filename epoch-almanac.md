# Epoch Almanac

*243-year Venus cycles. Octal era IDs; ascending transits (digits 8, 9) are skipped.*

---

- **Era `-30` (-4314 BCE) — The Roots of Complexity:** The independent, parallel emergence of complex settled society across every inhabited continent — from the Ubaid in Mesopotamia to Yangshao villages on the Yellow River to pastoralists in the green Sahara.
- **Era `-27` (-3828 BCE) — The First Temples:** The earliest monumental religious architecture — Eridu's successive temple rebuildings, Egypt's Hierakonpolis ceremonial center, and China's early ritual sites — the first structures built for collective spiritual purpose.
- **Era `-26` (-3585 BCE) — The Wheel & The Plow:** The three transformative inventions (wheel, potter's wheel, plow) that revolutionized transport, craft, and agriculture across Eurasia within this era.
- **Era `-25` (-3342 BCE) — The First Cities:** Uruk's rise to 80,000 people — and the simultaneous emergence of urban-scale settlements in Egypt, China, and the Indus Valley in parallel.
- **Era `-24` (-3099 BCE) — The Invention of Writing:** Writing's independent invention in Mesopotamia (cuneiform) and Egypt (hieroglyphs) within a century of each other, with script traditions also emerging in the Indus and China.
- **Era `-23` (-2856 BCE) — The First Nation-States:** The first political institutions — Egypt's unified bureaucracy, Mesopotamian city-state councils, and the simultaneous rise of state structures in China, the Indus, and Peru (Caral-Supe).
- **Era `-22` (-2613 BCE) — The Age of Pyramids & Grid Cities:** The Giza pyramids and the Indus grid-planned cities of Mohenjo-daro and Harappa — monumental architecture and urban planning at unprecedented scale on three continents.
- **Era `-21` (-2370 BCE) — The First Empires:** Sargon of Akkad's first multi-ethnic empire and Enheduanna, the first named author — plus parallel state formations in Egypt, the Indus, and China's Erlitou culture.
- **Era `-20` (-2127 BCE) — The First Urban Commonwealths:** Ur III's bureaucratic state, the *Gilgamesh* epic — humanity's first literature — and Stonehenge's final form as an astronomical observatory.
- **Era `-17` (-1884 BCE) — The Code of Justice:** Hammurabi's Code, which established that laws must be public, universal, and written in the common tongue — and Egypt's earliest philosophical literature.
- **Era `-16` (-1641 BCE) — The Dawn of Mathematics:** The Babylonian base-60 number system that gave us 60-minute hours and 360° circles, and Egyptian geometry — mathematics as a human invention.
- **Era `-15` (-1398 BCE) — The Age of Diplomacy & Revelation:** The Amarna letters (the first diplomatic archive), the Egyptian-Hittite peace treaty (the first between nations), and Akhenaten's first recorded monotheism.
- **Era `-14` (-1155 BCE) — The Bronze Age Commonwealth:** The interconnected late Bronze Age — Shang China, Mycenaean Greece, the Olmec in Mesoamerica, and the Ugaritic alphabet bridging writing systems.
- **Era `-13` (-912 BCE) — The Alphabet & The Oracle:** The Phoenician alphabet (ancestor of all modern alphabets), Homer's foundational epics, and the Olympic Games' invention of peaceful competition between rival peoples.
- **Era `-12` (-669 BCE) — The Axial Age Ignition:** The simultaneous, independent emergence of Confucius, the Buddha, Laozi, Mahavira, Thales, and Pythagoras — across civilizations, the same fundamental questions about reality and ethics.
- **Era `-11` (-426 BCE) — The Philosophical Dawn:** Plato, Aristotle, Ashoka, Eratosthenes, Euclid, Zeno, and Epicurus — the most concentrated burst of philosophical and scientific genius in human history.
- **Era `-10` (-184 BCE) — The Hellenistic Convergence:** The fusion of Greek, Persian, and Indian cultures, Hipparchus' discovery of precession, the Antikythera mechanism, and Roman natural law.
- **Era `-7` (0060 CE) — The First Universalisms:** Buddhism's spread along the Silk Road and Stoicism's teaching of universal human moral worth — ethics that transcended tribe and empire.
- **Era `-6` (0303 CE) — The Philosophical Bridge:** Neoplatonism and Augustine bridging classical philosophy with medieval theology, and Aryabhata proposing Earth's rotation — preserving ancient knowledge across cultural boundaries.
- **Era `-5` (0546 CE) — The Classical Codification:** Justinian's codification of Roman law, Brahmagupta's formalization of zero — the preservation and systematization of the classical heritage.
- **Era `-4` (0789 CE) — The Cosmopolitan Exchange:** The simultaneous flourishing of the Islamic Golden Age, Tang China, Heian Japan, and Viking exploration — a truly global intellectual world.
- **Era `-3` (1032 CE) — The Golden Synthesis:** Song dynasty innovations, Alhazen's empirical method, and the birth of universities — the cross-civilizational synthesis that set the stage for modern science.
- **Era `-2` (1275 CE) — The Polymath Awakening:** The rise of universities, Ibn Khaldun, Marco Polo, Dante, and Giotto — the emergence of individuals who mastered multiple fields of knowledge.
- **Era `-1` (1518 CE) — The Humanist Renaissance:** The heliocentric revolution, circumnavigation of the Earth, and the birth of the scientific method — humanity discovering its own cosmic context.
- **Era `0` (1761 CE) — The Enlightenment:** The 1761 Venus transit, the scientific method, human rights, electromagnetism, evolution, and germ theory — reason as a civilizing force.
- **Era `1` (2004 CE) — The Planetary Web:** The internet, the Human Genome Project, and Wikipedia — humanity gaining a single shared nervous system for the first time.
